# GoodFaith walkthrough

**Artifact status:** `walkthrough.mp4` is a visual storyboard draft, not the final Devpost upload. A final capture must show the live hosted portal in a browser; local browser capture is disabled in the current Work environment. The sequence below is the exact recording script.

## 2-minute demo sequence

1. Open the Overview page and introduce the problem: sponsors want to help, but need a trustworthy way to understand a request before money moves.
2. Open Maya’s request. Show the natural-language need, AI-prepared cart, item-level reasoning, and low-risk signals.
3. Click **Approve assistance**. Explain that AI prepares the transaction but a human remains in control.
4. Click **Create PayPal payment**. Show that GoodFaith creates a real PayPal Sandbox order and opens the buyer approval URL.
5. Approve with the Sandbox personal buyer account. Show PayPal returning to GoodFaith and the server capturing only after buyer approval.
6. Download the impact receipt. Show the AI reasoning, risk signals, cart, amount, PayPal order ID, capture ID, and Sandbox status.
7. Confirm fulfillment and show the completed transaction timeline.
8. Open **Requests** to show the platform queue and AI triage model.
9. Open **Impact ledger** to show verified outcome receipts.
10. Open **Settings** to show Sandbox mode, human approval, budget protection, and impact receipts.
11. Close with the message: GoodFaith makes trust visible without making help feel bureaucratic.

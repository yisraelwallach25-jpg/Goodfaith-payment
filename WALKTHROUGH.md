# GoodFaith walkthrough

**Artifact status:** `walkthrough.mp4` is a visual draft/title card only. It is not yet the final Devpost video because local browser capture is disabled in the current Work environment. The sequence below is the exact recording script for a real browser capture.

## 2-minute demo sequence

1. Open the Overview page and introduce the problem: sponsors want to help, but need a trustworthy way to understand a request before money moves.
2. Open Maya’s request. Show the natural-language need, AI-prepared cart, item-level reasoning, and low-risk signals.
3. Click **Approve assistance**. Explain that AI prepares the transaction but a human remains in control.
4. Click **Create PayPal payment**. In demo mode this creates a clearly labeled simulated Sandbox order; with PayPal credentials it is the server integration boundary.
5. Click **Confirm fulfillment**. Show the completed transaction timeline.
6. Open **Requests** to show the platform queue and AI triage model.
7. Open **Impact ledger** to show verified outcome receipts.
8. Open **Settings** to show Sandbox mode, human approval, budget protection, and impact receipts.
9. Close with the message: GoodFaith makes trust visible without making help feel bureaucratic.
